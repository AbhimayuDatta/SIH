#ASSAM_FLOOD_ISSUEE [[ASSAM FLOOD ALERT SYSTEM]][[Japan's Flood Alert Approaches]][[China's approch to the flood issue]]


|Aspect|China|Japan|
|---|---|---|
|Main public alert format|Four colours: blue, yellow, orange, red|Five action-oriented alert levels|
|Main emphasis|Administrative emergency response and flash-flood prevention|Clear evacuation action for residents|
|Small-catchment approach|Rainfall thresholds, local village warning, hazard investigation|River-specific forecasts, hazard maps, real-time risk grids|
|Forecasting|Rainfall, runoff, water level, soil moisture, and hydrological models|Radar, rain gauges, numerical forecasts, river models, and risk maps|
|Evacuation design|Often linked to local authorities and warning stages|Level 3 for people needing more time; Level 4 for everyone in danger|
|Communication|Government platforms, local warnings, and meteorological warnings|JMA, municipalities, media, internet, public alerts, and local authorities|
