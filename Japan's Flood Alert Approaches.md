#ASSAM_FLOOD_ISSUEE [[ASSAM FLOOD ALERT SYSTEM]]
## 1. Five-levels of Alert 

| Japan alert level | General action |
|---|---|
| Level 1 | Increase awareness and monitor information |
| Level 2 | Check hazard maps, evacuation centres, and routes |
| Level 3 | Elderly people and people needing more time should evacuate |
| Level 4 | Everyone in dangerous areas should evacuate |
| Level 5 | A disaster is occurring or imminent; immediately secure safety |
