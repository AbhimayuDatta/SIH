**Idea**
The core concept would be similar to the refrigeration stock management system but instead of food items there will be clothes. It will keep check of the clothes you have in the closet and the ones the laundry and ones you are currently wearing all this will be visible in an app. 

**Problem Statement**
This will obviously help in wardrobe management but it will help us through the choice paralysis that we suffer when selecting an outfit because you can  easily do that by just configuring your outfit before hand through the app.
