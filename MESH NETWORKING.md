# Which sensor to use ?

### Types of water level sensors -

1. **Submersible hydrostatic pressure sensor** - 
	- **Working** - Liquid Level Increases ---> Hydrostatic Pressure Increases ---> Diaphragm Deflects ---> Strain Gauges Detect Deformation ---> Wheatstone Bridge Produces Voltage ---> Signal Conditioning Electronics ---> 4-20 mA / Voltage / Digital Output ---> Displayed as Tank Level.
	- **Limitations** - Not Ideal for river bcuz Vent  tube blocking can cause errors and requires temperature compensation for accuracy.
2. **Ultrasonic level sensor** - 
	- **Working** - Liquid Surface ---> Echo Reflection ---> Ultrasonic Pulse ---> Piezoelectric Transducer ---> Transmitter Electronics ---> Microcontroller ---> Time Measurement ---> Distance Calculation ---> Level Calculation ---> 4-20 mA / Digital Output.
	- **Limitations** - Needs a Solid area to mount and any obstacle could easily mess up the readings since it measures the time of the travel of the sound wave.
